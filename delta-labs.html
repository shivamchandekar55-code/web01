The Person Behind It

Founder, Delta Labs

I started Delta Labs because I kept seeing the same problem: small business owners working harder than they needed to, not because they lacked drive, but because they lacked the right tools.

Big tech agencies were not an option. Too expensive, too complex, built for companies ten times their size. I wanted to change that.

My goal is simple: give every small business access to the same quality of technology that large companies take for granted, built around how your business actually works, at a price that makes sense.
